How it works

Tap the number of pins you knocked down, or hit the big strike / spare button. Frames score themselves once their bonus balls are thrown — a – means the frame is still waiting on a bonus. Max possible assumes you strike out from here, so it drops the moment a strike or spare is missed and tells you what is still on the table.
